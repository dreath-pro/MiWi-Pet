MiWi Pet

🛠️ Technical Highlights & Mechanics
A simple clicker game where you hatch and collect pets, whether you buy eggs from shop or through trade, this project features an RNG and probability of hatching rarer pets and obtaining rare eggs
you can also get through trade from an NPC

🧰 Tech Stack
IDE: Android Studio
Language: Java
Art: Bing Image Creator AI

🚀 How to Play
You can first buy egg from the shop and hatch your first pet and you'll earn from hatching, which your inventory grows overtime
